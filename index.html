Three friends, one shared table

PaperPeace began at NUST, somewhere between deadlines and late-night tea, when we realised the pages we reached for said as much about our state of mind as anything we wrote on them.

We design every notebook, sticker sheet and tape roll to slow you down for a moment — a small, calm ritual in an otherwise loud day.
